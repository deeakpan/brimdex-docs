# Product Overview

This section explains Brimdex from the product and user flow point of view.

## What users do on Brimdex

Users normally interact with Brimdex in one of three ways:

1. **Trade** BOUND or BREAK
2. **Provide launch liquidity** through the vault flow
3. **Redeem** after settlement

## What makes the product distinct

- the market is quoted continuously while it is live
- the orderbook gives secondary-market exits
- launch and settlement are automated through Somnia-native infrastructure
- onchain agents help resolve markets after expiry
- settlement is intentionally anchored on Somnia, even when users arrive through multichain routing
