# Trading Overview

Brimdex gives users two trading paths:

- **AMM trading** for instant execution
- **orderbook trading** for price-specific execution

## Why there are two paths

They serve different needs.

**AMM trading** is for users who want:

- instant entry
- simple execution
- immediate access to BOUND or BREAK

**Orderbook trading** is for users who want:

- early exit
- tighter pricing when liquidity is available
- control over their limit price

## Simple mental model

Think of Brimdex trading like this:

- use the **AMM** when you want to trade now
- use the **orderbook** when you want to trade at your price
